## Proposed addition after Installation work

### Included, provisional or excluded?

A low total is not a like-for-like comparison when one proposal leaves work for you to arrange. For each item below, ask the installer to label it included, provisional, excluded or not applicable. Record the document and date supporting the answer.

| Item | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Electrical work | Which supply, cabling and connection work is included, and who confirms the final scope after the survey? |
| Base and drainage | Who provides the base and agreed drainage arrangements, and are preparatory works priced? |
| Radiators and pipework | Which rooms and changes are included, and where is the design evidence supporting them? |
| Removal and making good | What is removed, disposed of and reinstated, and what must the homeowner arrange? |
| Provisional allowances | What does the allowance cover, what remains uncertain, and how will any additional price be agreed before work? |

Suggested request: Please identify the status of each item and point me to its written scope. For anything provisional or excluded, explain who is responsible and how the final work and cost will be agreed.

Do not treat an unanswered question as proof of poor workmanship or an incorrect price. HQC checks documents; it cannot certify design, inspect the property or establish a fair price.
