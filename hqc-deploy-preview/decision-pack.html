Know the limits of a document check

Scope labels describe extracted statements, not independent verification of the work. Supporting documents may contain details not captured by extraction. Check the original wording and ask for written clarification.

The pack does not inspect your home, certify a design, approve an installer or guarantee running costs, performance or grant eligibility.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need three quotes?

No. With one quote, the pack summarises extracted scope, evidence gaps and questions. With two or three, it also presents them side by side.

Does Not stated mean the work is excluded?

No. It means the detail was not found in the extracted fields. Check the original documents and ask the installer.

Will you choose or contact an installer?

No. The decision and any installer conversation remain yours.

Does this cover Solar/Battery or EV?

This pack is for heat-pump quotes. Other supported technologies retain their separate free-check journeys.
