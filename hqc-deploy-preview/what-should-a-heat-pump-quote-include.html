What Should a Heat Pump Quote Include?

A useful heat-pump quote should explain what will be installed, why it has been proposed and what you will pay. Supporting documents may hold some of the detail. Together, they should give you a clear picture of the work and any decisions still outstanding.

A clear equipment specification

Specific equipment details make comparisons more meaningful.

  • Exact heat-pump make and model.
  • Cylinder details, where applicable.
  • Controls.
  • Major accessories or supporting equipment.
  • Proposed locations.

The basis of the design

The quote may reference a separate design report. Ask for it if it has not been supplied.

  • Heat loss for the property and individual rooms.
  • Design indoor and outdoor temperatures.
  • Heating flow temperature.
  • Radiator or underfloor-heating suitability.
  • The basis for selecting the proposed heat pump.

An itemised description of the work

For each significant item, establish who handles it and whether it is included, excluded or still provisional.

  • Equipment installation.
  • Radiator and pipework changes.
  • Electrical work.
  • Old-system removal.
  • External works and access.
  • Commissioning, making good and handover.

A price you can interpret

If funding is assumed, ask what happens if eligibility or the award differs from the proposal.

  • Total installation price.
  • Taxes included or separately stated, as applicable.
  • Funding or discount shown separately.
  • The amount you are expected to pay.
  • Allowances and optional extras.
  • Circumstances that could change the price.

Practical terms

  • Quote validity.
  • Deposit and payment stages.
  • Cancellation terms.
  • Indicative installation timing.
  • Responsibilities before work starts.
  • Warranty and aftercare arrangements.

A useful clarification request

“Please provide an updated scope showing included work, exclusions and provisional allowances, together with the supporting design documents and payment schedule.”

Frequently asked questions

Is a short quote always a bad quote?

No. It may refer to detailed supporting documents. Ask for the complete proposal.

What does subject to survey mean?

Some details may change after assessment. Ask which parts remain uncertain and when the final design and price will be confirmed.

Are radiator upgrades always required?

No. Their suitability depends on each room’s heat requirement and the proposed system temperatures. Ask for the assessment.

Can an omitted item be treated as included?

Do not assume that it is. Request written confirmation.
