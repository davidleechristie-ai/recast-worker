# SOP — Weekly strategy

Aggregate seven days of vault evidence. Compare genuine cumulative revenue, purchases, qualified acquisition and each measurable journey stage with prior week where possible. Classify experiments without moving baselines. Identify the dominant constraint to the £100 validation target, kill/defer low-value work, and rank backlog by expected revenue impact × probability × speed-to-evidence / effort+cost+dependency risk. Set no more than three measurable objectives.
