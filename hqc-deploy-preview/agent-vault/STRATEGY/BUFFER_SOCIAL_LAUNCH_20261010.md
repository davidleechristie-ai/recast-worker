### 01 — The headline price is not the whole quote
Format: text / optional 3-panel checklist graphic.
Copy:
Two installer quotes can have the same headline price — and cover very different things.

Before choosing, check:
• What's actually included?
• What might cost extra?
• Which assumptions are being made?
• What's written down about warranty and aftercare?

A cheaper quote isn't necessarily the better value. Check the detail before you commit.

### 02 — Five things to check on a heat pump quote
Format: carousel, 5 slides.
Copy:
Got a heat pump installation quote? Don't just compare the bottom line.

Five questions worth asking:
1. Which heat pump model and capacity are specified?
2. What's included for radiators and other heating upgrades?
3. Which controls and hot-water components are included?
4. What electrical or building work might be extra?
5. What commissioning and aftercare will you receive?

Format: short text.
Copy:
Three words to find in every installer quote: **"Not included."**

Before you accept a home energy quote, ask for any unclear exclusions in writing.
