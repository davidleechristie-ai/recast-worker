Is This a Good Heat Pump Quote?

A clear price is useful. A clear explanation of what you are buying is just as important. A good quote should help you understand the proposed system, the work included and the evidence behind the design. You should also be able to see what remains unresolved before committing.

1. Can you identify the proposed system?

“Heat pump installation” alone leaves important details open.

  • Manufacturer and exact model.
  • Proposed output or capacity.
  • Hot-water cylinder details, where relevant.
  • Controls included.
  • Main installation locations.

2. Can you see why it has been proposed?

A document checker can identify missing evidence. It cannot replace a site survey or independently certify the design.

  • The heat-loss calculation.
  • The design temperatures used.
  • The proposed heating flow temperature.
  • The radiator or emitter assessment.
  • An explanation of required upgrades.

3. Is the scope specific?

If you cannot answer from the supplied documents, send those questions back.

  • Which radiators will change?
  • What pipework is included?
  • Who handles electrical work?
  • Who removes the old system?
  • What making good is covered?

4. Are the costs understandable?

An estimate with unresolved costs is not directly equivalent to a fully specified fixed-price proposal.

  • Total price and your contribution.
  • Grant assumptions.
  • Allowances and provisional sums.
  • Excluded work.
  • Deposit, payment stages and quote validity.

5. Do you know what happens afterwards?

The equipment matters, but so do system design, installation scope and aftercare.

  • Commissioning and handover arrangements.
  • Instructions for using the controls.
  • Warranty terms.
  • Servicing requirements.
  • A clear contact for installation problems.

Turn the check into a next step

Missing information is a reason to ask questions, not proof that a system will perform badly.

  • Clear enough to compare: important details are documented.
  • Needs clarification: written answers are needed before comparing or committing.
  • Needs further design evidence: the documents do not yet explain the proposed system.

Frequently asked questions

Can you tell me whether the price is fair?

Price depends on the property, design and installation scope. Start by establishing what the price covers; a headline figure alone cannot establish value.

Does a well-known brand make it a good quote?

No single brand establishes the quality of the whole proposal. Look at the design, scope and support.

What if my quote is only an estimate?

You can still check it. Treat unresolved details as questions to settle before a final commitment.

Can the free check guarantee performance?

No. It reviews the supplied information and highlights gaps. It does not guarantee comfort, running costs or installation quality.
