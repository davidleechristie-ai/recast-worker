# Home Quote Check commercial north star

## North star
Sustain at least **£1,000 genuine monthly revenue** with minimal human operation.

## Immediate milestones
- First genuine £4.99 purchase.
- £100 cumulative genuine revenue within the original three-month validation window.
- £250/month.
- £500/month.
- £1,000/month sustained.

At £4.99, 21 purchases would exceed £100 gross and about 201 purchases would exceed £1,000 gross in a month, before refunds/fees/tax. These are planning arithmetic, not forecasts.

## Revenue-first operating rule
Until first genuine revenue exists, first-customer acquisition and conversion outrank general product polish, feature breadth and additional low-evidence content unless production, analysis correctness, trust or checkout is broken. SEO remains important but is one acquisition channel, not the strategy.

Work: qualified homeowner with existing quote(s) → understands independent value → checker start → upload/manual entry → genuine analysis → second quote/comparison → Decision Pack value → checkout → genuine purchase → referral/repeat.

Refresh authoritative payment, acquisition, durable funnel, Search Console/search and production-health evidence first. Exclude synthetic activity. Unavailable metrics remain null.

## Parallel workstreams
Progress independent non-confounding work without waiting for another experiment to mature:
1. qualified high-intent acquisition/distribution;
2. technical SEO/indexing and substantive existing-quote decision content;
3. single-quote activation/upload completion;
4. multi-quote progression/correctness;
5. £4.99 paid boundary and checkout reliability;
6. privacy-safe sharing/referral;
7. anonymous revenue/funnel instrumentation;
8. production/mobile/desktop reliability.

Prioritise by expected revenue impact × probability × speed-to-evidence divided by effort + cost + dependency risk.

## Product and independence guardrails
HQC is independent evidence/comparison support. Nominal kW, installer claims, automated extraction, MCS references and BUS references are not design certification, installer certification or grant eligibility. Do not materially expand personal-data collection for conversion. Keep referral actions separate from analysis and never alter results for commercial reasons.

## Deployment
Measure → Diagnose → Research → Prioritise → Build → Test → Commit → Preview/Canary → Verify → Production → Measure. Use GitHub/Cloudflare and Worker hqc-production. Never AppDeploy. Preserve custom-domain routing. UI releases require rendered desktop/mobile verification.
