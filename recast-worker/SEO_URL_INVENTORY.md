## Guardrails

Every page must have a unique title, description, H1, intent paragraph, example and limitation. Every page must point to a real Recast capability and an appropriate commercial next step. Browser-local tools and hosted API/Automation processing must remain explicitly distinct. Unsupported combinations, generated search-result pages, doorway pages, duplicate intent pages and claims that are not present in the product are not eligible for indexable publication.
