What the regulator recorded

The published CAR for Land Adj To Millhouse Garage records eight permit breaches and a total compliance score of 82.1. Why a supplier may investigate

Multiple operational and management-system findings can create a reason for relevant suppliers to research whether the operator needs compliance support, process changes, training, site infrastructure or records/reporting capability. This is an inference, not evidence that the operator intends to buy.
