Evidence before inference

From regulatory change to commercial signal.

ScrapSignal is designed to surface moments when an operator change could create supplier demand, while keeping the underlying evidence separate from the commercial interpretation.

1. Detect

Monitor authoritative public registers and regulatory sources for meaningful changes.

2. Classify

Separate potentially commercial events such as new sites, new operators, moves, permit changes and compliance actions from administrative noise.

3. Interpret

Map the observed event to plausible purchasing requirements and relevant supplier categories.

4. Evidence-link

Preserve the authoritative source so commercial teams can verify why a signal exists before acting.

What ScrapSignal treats as a strong event

  • NEW_SITE — a newly observed licensed operating location that may need systems, equipment and supplier setup.
  • NEW_OPERATOR — a newly observed operator before incumbent supplier relationships are established.
  • EXPANSION_OR_MOVE — operational change that may create implementation or replacement demand.
  • PERMIT_CHANGE — a regulatory change that may require process, equipment or compliance work.
  • COMPLIANCE_ACTION / ASSESSMENT — evidence of a corrective requirement that can create a time-sensitive buying need.

Evidence is not the same as purchase certainty

ScrapSignal does not claim that every regulatory event results in a purchase. The source event is factual; the likely supplier requirement is a commercial interpretation. Signals are prioritised by evidence strength, urgency, likely expenditure and supplier relevance so sales teams can decide where to investigate first.

Operating principle: no invented customers, no synthetic traction and no fabricated regulatory events. Illustrative examples are labelled as illustrative.

Built for supplier timing, not database volume

A static database answers who operates in the sector. ScrapSignal is intended to answer what changed recently, why it may matter commercially and which supplier category could benefit from acting early.
