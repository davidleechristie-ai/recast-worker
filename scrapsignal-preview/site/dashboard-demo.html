Opportunity workflow

2. Weak/non-commercial changes are filtered out.
3. Remaining changes are scored and matched to likely supplier categories.
4. Your team receives the fact, commercial assessment, evidence and suggested opening question.
5. Save prospects to Watchlist or export the filtered list for your sales workflow.
