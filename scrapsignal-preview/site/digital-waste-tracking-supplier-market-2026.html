Why static operator lists lose value in this market

Defra estimates approximately 12,000 waste site operators across the UK. A generic database can tell competing vendors who many of those organisations are. It does not tell a salesperson which account changed recently, what changed or whether that change creates a plausible buying window.

ScrapSignal's thesis: authoritative operational change is a stronger starting point for sales prioritisation than repeatedly prospecting the same undifferentiated operator list.

Examples include a newly observed licensed site or operator. The source establishes the change; ScrapSignal separately interprets what the change may mean for software, weighing, site equipment or compliance suppliers.
